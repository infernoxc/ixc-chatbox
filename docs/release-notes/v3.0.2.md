**IXC 3.0.2** fixes bugs found after 3.0.1:
- **Updates install by themselves** from now on, never while you stream or record (Settings > General to turn it off).
- Sending a reply to **ALL** no longer shows errors for platforms you haven't set up in IXC; it still goes everywhere it can.
- **YouTube no longer goes online and offline during a stream.** Once IXC finds your stream it stays connected, like Twitch and Kick.
- **YouTube no longer shows "offline" while you're live** when Streamer.bot is connected to YouTube: IXC takes the live stream from
  Streamer.bot when YouTube's page doesn't show it, and keeps Streamer.bot's viewer number.
- **YouTube** finds your live stream in more cases, and says the real reason when it can't: "stream scheduled, not started yet",
  or that YouTube is asking your network to confirm it's not a bot (this happens on some networks and VPNs).
- **Quick connect** downloads `cloudflared.exe` again if the copy on your PC is damaged or blocked.

Use this version rather than 3.0.1 or 3.0.0. Updating keeps all your settings.

**IXC 3.0** joins IXC ChatBox and IXC Music into one program for OBS. It reads chat on its own, so **Streamer.bot is no longer
needed**, and everything is set up from one window.

### Highlights
- **One install, one window.** Express setup connects OBS, adds the panels and the audio sources, and opens a short first-run guide.
  Advanced setup lets you choose each step.
- **Built-in chat** for Twitch, Kick, YouTube and Rumble. Each platform has its own status: connected, reconnecting, offline or
  needs sign-in. One platform failing never affects the others. Streamer.bot still works as an optional source.
- **Connect account** (Twitch, YouTube, Kick) to reply in chat, get follower alerts and viewer counts. Reading chat needs no sign-in.
- **Chat filters** (links, caps, spam, banned words…), each set to *Off*, *Don't read it out* or *Hide on stream*.
- **Command manager** with permissions, cooldowns, platforms and variables. Built-in `!np`, `!queue`, `!skip`, `!uptime`, `!wrongsong`,
  `!ttsskip`, `!ttson`, `!ttsoff`, `!viewers`.
- **Song requests** with permissions, cooldowns, queue and per-person limits, longest song, account age and banned songs/artists.
- **Music:** a queue kept in IXC that survives restarts, local music folders, YouTube and Spotify links, autoplay, Now Playing text,
  and per-platform OBS audio tracks.
- **Chat voice:** voices per person, per role and per platform; pause/resume; music **ducking** that always restores the volume.
- **Overlays** (now playing, queue, chat, viewers, chat voice, alerts, status) with a visual editor and one-click **Add to OBS**.
- **Viewer counter** that shows when a number is stale instead of showing an old number.
- **Phone remote with no setup:** press **Show QR code** and scan it. Quick connect opens a temporary secure link through
  Cloudflare (no account, works on mobile data); one-time QR codes; a list of phones with rename/disconnect/remove. An optional
  permanent relay keeps phones paired across restarts.
- **Chat panel and phone:** viewers watching and people chatting at a glance; type `!` for every IXC and Streamer.bot command;
  replies go through Streamer.bot for platforms you haven't signed in to. Twitch viewer numbers without signing in.
- **System check** with **Fix automatically**, settings backups, diagnostics export without secrets, safe updates with rollback, and
  crash restart without restart loops.

### Upgrading from IXC ChatBox 2.x or IXC Music 2.x
Run `IXC-Setup-v3.0.2.exe`. Settings are moved over and backed up, passwords and keys move into the encrypted store, the music queue is
taken over, and the old tunnel program is removed. Phones need to scan a new QR code once. See [UNINSTALL.md](../UNINSTALL.md).

### Tested
- Automated, on every push (Linux): integration tests run IXC against fake Twitch, Kick, YouTube, Rumble, OBS, Streamer.bot and
  GitHub servers and against the real relay code in Cloudflare's local runtime; browser tests drive the dashboard, docks, overlays
  and phone page in Chromium.
- Automated, on every push (Windows): build with Windows' own compiler, server and DPAPI checks, install/update/crash recovery/uninstall
  with the script and with the real `IXC-Setup.exe` on a clean machine. Live checks against the real platforms are reported but don't
  fail the build.

### Not tested automatically: please check by hand before a release
1. Real OBS 30+: Express setup adds the panels, the **IXC Audio** scene and its two sources; music and chat voice are audible; ducking
   lowers and restores the music; per-platform audio routing goes to the chosen tracks.
2. Real accounts: **Connect account** for Twitch, YouTube and Kick; replying from the chat panel on each; signing out.
3. Chat on a live channel for each platform, including emotes, deletions/bans, and a YouTube stream that ends and a new one starting.
4. Quick connect on real Windows: the first Show QR code downloads cloudflared (signature check), the QR opens on a phone on mobile
   data, chat/music/voice work, Stop Quick connect disconnects it. If a relay is deployed: pair, restart the PC, see the phone reconnect.
5. The tray icon menu (Open, System check, Connect phone, Restart, Open logs, Quit) and **Open IXC with Windows**.
6. Windows SmartScreen on the downloaded installer (unsigned) and installing from a non-admin account.
7. An update from the previous release through **Update now**.

### Known limitations
- The publisher must deploy the relay and register the Twitch, Google and Kick apps once ([DEVELOPER-SETUP.md](../DEVELOPER-SETUP.md)).
  Until then, **Connect account** shows that it isn't available (replies still work through Streamer.bot); the phone remote uses
  Quick connect. Chat reading, music, voices and overlays work.
- Until Google verifies the app, YouTube sign-in shows an "unverified app" warning and allows at most 100 users.
- The installer isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn.
- Kick chat reading, YouTube chat reading, Twitch viewer numbers without sign-in and the neural voices use public but undocumented
  endpoints that can change. Quick connect depends on Cloudflare's free quick tunnels (no uptime guarantee). The offline
  Windows voice takes over when the neural voices fail.
- Rumble has no sign-in: reading chat uses your personal Live Stream API link, and IXC can't reply on Rumble.
