## 📘 Complete setup, step by step (new to OBS, IXC and Streamer.bot)

Do the parts **in order**. Everything is free. You never need developer apps or client IDs.

| Program | What it does |
|---|---|
| **OBS Studio** | Captures game, camera and sound, encodes the video, sends it to the platforms |
| **IXC** (this release) | Chat box for all platforms, music player, chat voice, commands, timers, clips, system check |
| **Streamer.bot** | Holds your Twitch / YouTube / Kick sign-ins so IXC can **reply** in chat; runs automations |
| **Aitum Multistream** (OBS plugin) | Sends the same video to more platforms at once |

> 🔒 **Never share your stream keys or passwords.** Paste them only into the fields named below.

---

### Part 1 – OBS Studio

1. Download **OBS Studio 30 or newer** from [obsproject.com](https://obsproject.com) > **Windows** > install > open it once.
2. **Settings** > **Output** > **Output Mode: Advanced** > tab **Streaming**: **Video Encoder** = your graphics card
   (*AMD HW H.264*, *NVIDIA NVENC H.264* or *QuickSync H.264*), **Rate Control: CBR**, **Bitrate: 6000**,
   **Keyframe Interval: 2**, **Audio Track: 1**.
3. **Settings** > **Output** > tab **Replay Buffer**: tick **Enable Replay Buffer**, **Maximum Replay Time: 120** s.
   **Settings** > **General**: tick **Automatically start replay buffer when streaming**.
4. **Settings** > **Hotkeys** > **Save Replay**: click the box and press **F10** (saves the last 120 s as a clip).
5. **Tools** > **WebSocket Server Settings**: tick **Enable WebSocket server**, **Server Port: 4455**, tick
   **Enable Authentication** > **Show Connect Info** > copy the **Server Password** (you need it in Part 3, step 7) > **OK**.
6. **File** > **Exit** (close OBS before installing IXC).

### Part 2 – IXC

1. Below, under **Assets**, download **`IXC-Setup-v3.3.0.exe`** and double-click it.
   **"Windows protected your PC"?** **More info** > **Run anyway** (no paid certificate; checksum in `SHA256SUMS.txt`).
2. Click **Express setup**. The **setup assistant** opens in your browser: type your channel names (Twitch name,
   YouTube @handle, Kick name). It sets up OBS (WebSocket, IXC panels, music and chat-voice sources) and tests everything.
3. Open OBS > menu **Docks** > tick **IXC**, **IXC ChatBox**, **IXC Music**.
4. IXC dashboard (tray icon, bottom right > **Open**) > **Updates & diagnostics** > **System check**: all green.
   Red item? Press its **Fix** button.

   ![IXC ChatBox dock](https://github.com/infernoxc/ixc-chatbox/raw/claude/project-thread-o8dbuw/docs/images/chatbox-dock.png) ![IXC Music dock](https://github.com/infernoxc/ixc-chatbox/raw/claude/project-thread-o8dbuw/docs/images/music-dock.png)

### Part 3 – Streamer.bot (download, sign in, connect to OBS and IXC)

**Download and start**
1. Go to the official site **[streamer.bot](https://streamer.bot)** > **Download** > download the ZIP.
2. Right-click the ZIP > **Extract All…** > choose a folder you keep, for example **`C:\Streamer.bot`** (not Downloads).
   ⚠ Keep **only one** Streamer.bot folder. Two copies cause confusion (updates land in the wrong one).
3. Open that folder > double-click **`Streamer.bot.exe`**. If Windows asks, **More info** > **Run anyway**.

**Sign in to your platforms**

4. **Twitch**: **Platforms** > **Twitch** > **Accounts** > **Broadcaster Account** > **Connect** > log in to Twitch in the browser >
   **Authorize**. Optional: a second Twitch account for a chat bot under **Bot Account** > **Connect**.
5. **YouTube**: **Platforms** > **YouTube** > **Accounts** > **Broadcaster** > **Connect** > choose your Google account >
   **Allow**.
6. **Kick** (needs the free **Kick.bot** extension):
   - Download the latest **`Kick.bot-vX.Y.Z.zip`** from the Kick.bot project page.
   - Close Streamer.bot. Open the ZIP and copy **all its `.dll` files** (Kick.bot.dll, PusherClient.dll, WebSocket4Net.dll, …)
     into the **`dlls`** folder **inside your Streamer.bot folder** (e.g. `C:\Streamer.bot\dlls`). Replace existing files.
   - Start Streamer.bot again. In the **Kick.bot** window, log in with your Kick account.
   - A **"Kick.bot – Update available"** window means the `dlls\Kick.bot.dll` that Streamer.bot loads is old. Copy the new ZIP's
     DLLs into **that** `dlls` folder (the one next to the `Streamer.bot.exe` you start).

**Connect Streamer.bot to OBS**

7. **Stream Apps** > **OBS** > right-click in the list > **Add**:
   **Name** `OBS`, **Host** `127.0.0.1`, **Port** `4455`, **Password** = the OBS WebSocket password from Part 1 step 5,
   tick **Auto Connect** and **Reconnect** > **OK** > right-click the entry > **Connect**. Status turns **Connected**.

**Connect Streamer.bot to IXC**

8. **Servers/Clients** > **WebSocket Server**: **Address** `127.0.0.1`, **Port** `8080`, **Endpoint** `/`, tick **Auto Start**,
   tick **Enable Authentication** and type a password > **Start Server**.
9. In IXC: **System** > **Streamer.bot**: **Address** `ws://127.0.0.1:8080/`. IXC reads the password from Streamer.bot's
   settings by itself. If it can't, paste the password from step 8 > **Save**. Press **Test connection**: it shows your accounts.
   **Start with IXC**: **No** (you open Streamer.bot yourself). Now IXC replies in chat through Streamer.bot.

### Part 4 – Twitch + Kick + YouTube at once (smooth: one encode)

1. Install **Aitum Multistream** from [aitum.tv](https://aitum.tv) with OBS closed. Open OBS > **Docks** > **Aitum Multistream**.
2. OBS **Settings** > **Stream**: **Service: Twitch** > **Connect Account** (log in) *or* **Use Stream Key** and paste the key from
   Twitch Creator Dashboard > Settings > Stream.
3. Aitum dock > ⚙ > **Add output**:
   - **Kick**: Server = *Stream URL*, Key = *Stream Key* (Kick dashboard > Settings > Stream), **Video Encoder: Main Encoder**,
     **Audio Track: 2**.
   - **YouTube**: Server `rtmps://a.rtmps.youtube.com:443/live2`, Key = YouTube Studio > Create > Go live > Stream key,
     **Video Encoder: Main Encoder**, **Audio Track: 3**.
4. IXC > **Streams**: Twitch, Kick and YouTube show **READY**. IXC > **Audio routing**: choose where music plays.

### Part 5 – Vertical clips for Shorts / TikTok (optional)

1. Install **Aitum Vertical Canvas** from [aitum.tv](https://aitum.tv) with OBS closed.
2. Vertical dock > ⚙ > **Backtrack**: enable, **90–120 s**, choose a folder > **Save Backtrack Hotkey**: press **F9**.
3. While live: **F9** = vertical clip, **F10** = horizontal clip.

### Going live, every time

1. Open **Streamer.bot**, then **OBS**. IXC is already running in the tray.
2. IXC **System check**: all green.
3. OBS **Start Streaming** (Twitch) **first**. Then in the Aitum dock **Start** Kick, then **Start** YouTube.
4. Ending: Aitum **Stop** both, then OBS **Stop Streaming**.

### If something goes wrong

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| OBS "Failed to connect to server" | Settings > Stream: key empty or wrong. Paste it again. |
| Aitum "main was not started" | Press OBS **Start Streaming** first. |
| Laggy stream, GPU 100 % | Kick/YouTube must use **Main Encoder** (one encode). |
| IXC panels say "click here to retry" | IXC > Settings > **Start and stop with OBS: off**. |
| IXC can't reach Streamer.bot | Streamer.bot > Servers/Clients > WebSocket Server > **Start Server**; IXC > Streamer.bot > **Test connection**. |
| Streamer.bot keeps reopening | IXC > Streamer.bot > **Start with IXC: No**. |
| "Kick.bot – Update available" every time | Copy the new Kick.bot DLLs into the `dlls` folder of the Streamer.bot you actually start (Part 3 step 6). |

## What's new in 3.3.0

- **Complete beginner setup** above, including Streamer.bot (download, Twitch / YouTube / Kick via Kick.bot, connect to OBS and IXC).
- **Streams page**: Twitch, YouTube and Kick at a glance (NOT CONFIGURED / READY / ERROR / STREAMING), read from OBS and Aitum.
- **Fixed**: "OBS has no source called IXC Music Player" after renaming IXC's sources in OBS. Audio routing now follows renames.
- **Streamer.bot**: choose whether IXC closes it when IXC quits; IXC asks before stopping it while you're live.
- **Simpler**: Multistream Studio and Vertical Studio are gone from the menu (use the Aitum plugins in OBS). Developer-app setup is
  only in Advanced mode; replies go through Streamer.bot.
- **YouTube**: IXC looks for your live stream when OBS goes live instead of checking every 30 seconds.

**Already have IXC?** Install the same way, or wait: IXC updates itself when you're not live. Your settings stay (a backup is made first).
