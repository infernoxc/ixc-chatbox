## 📘 Completely new to OBS, IXC or Streamer.bot?

Follow the step-by-step guide (every click written out): [**First-time setup**](https://github.com/infernoxc/ixc-chatbox/blob/v3.3.0/docs/FIRST-TIME-SETUP.md)

## ⬇ Install IXC in 5 steps

1. Install **OBS Studio 30 or newer** from [obsproject.com](https://obsproject.com), open it once, then close it (File > Exit).
2. Below, under **Assets**, click **`IXC-Setup-v3.3.0.exe`** to download it.
3. Open your **Downloads** folder and double-click **`IXC-Setup-v3.3.0.exe`**.
   - **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. (IXC has no paid signing certificate. The file's
     checksum is in `SHA256SUMS.txt` below.)
4. Click **Express setup**. If OBS is open, close it when the setup asks.
5. IXC opens a **setup assistant** in your browser: follow it. It sets up OBS, asks for your channel names and tests everything.
   Then open OBS: the IXC panels are under **Docks**, and IXC sits in the tray (bottom right).

**Without the setup program:** download **`IXC-Suite-v3.3.0.zip`**, right-click it > **Extract All…** > **Extract**, then
double-click **`Install.bat`** in the extracted folder.

**Already have IXC?** Install it the same way, or wait: IXC updates itself when you're not live. Your settings, accounts, queue
and playlists stay (a backup is made first).

## Streaming to Twitch, YouTube and Kick at once (recommended setup)

IXC works with the **Aitum Multistream** OBS plugin. To keep your PC smooth, let OBS encode the video **once**:
- OBS > Settings > Stream: your main platform (for example Twitch: **Connect Account** or paste its stream key).
- OBS > Settings > Output > Streaming: your hardware encoder (e.g. AMD/NVIDIA H.264), CBR, keyframe interval 2 s.
- Aitum dock > settings: add the other platforms with **Video Encoder = Main Encoder**.
- Go live: press OBS **Start Streaming** first, then start the other platforms in the Aitum dock.
- Check IXC > **Streams**: each platform shows READY when its server and key are set (keys are never shown).
