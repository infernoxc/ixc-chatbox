## ⬇ Install or update

1. Below, under **Assets**, click **`IXC-Suite-v3.3.2.zip`** to download it.
2. Close OBS. Open your **Downloads** folder, right-click the zip > **Extract All…** > **Extract**.
3. In the extracted folder, double-click **`Install.bat`**. No admin rights needed.
   - **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. (The checksum is in `SHA256SUMS.txt` below.)

Your settings, accounts, queue and playlists stay (a backup is made first). **Going back:** install v3.3.1 the same way.
New to IXC? Follow the [first-time setup guide](https://github.com/infernoxc/ixc-chatbox/blob/main/docs/FIRST-TIME-SETUP.md).

## What's new in 3.3.2

New dashboard page: **🎁 Rewards & sounds** (menu > Automation).

### 🎁 Channel point rewards (Twitch and Kick)
- Make rewards in IXC: title, cost, description, whether the viewer types text, cooldown, colour, Twitch and/or Kick.
- What a reward does: **request a song** (the viewer types it), **run any chat command** (`!skip`, `!previous`, `!shuffle`,
  `!volume 40`, `!pause`…, even moderator-only ones), **play a sound effect**, **read the viewer's message aloud**, or **post a chat message**.
- **+ Add ready-made rewards** adds 9 common ones (song request, skip, previous, shuffle, volume, pause, sound, read aloud, hydrate).
- **Twitch:** **⟳ Create / update on Twitch & Kick** makes the rewards on your channel. Connect Twitch again once (Platforms & Accounts)
  so IXC gets the reward permission. Channel points need **Affiliate or Partner**; until then IXC keeps your rewards ready and says so.
  If a song request can't be added, the viewer gets the points back.
- **Kick:** create rewards with **exactly the same titles** in your Kick dashboard (Channel Points), or turn on **Let IXC create Kick rewards**
  and connect Kick again. IXC picks up claims from Kick's chat connection, from Streamer.bot / Kick.bot, or from any tool that calls
  `POST http://localhost:8767/api/rewards/redeem`. The same claim arriving twice counts once.

### 🔊 Sound effects (20 slots)
- Your own sound files (mp3, wav, ogg, m4a): type the path or upload (up to 1 MB). Each has a name, volume and cooldown.
- Viewers play them with a reward, or with `!sound name` in chat (`!sounds` lists them). You choose who may use the chat command.
- They play through the **IXC TTS** source in OBS, so viewers hear them too, and the music goes quieter meanwhile.

### 📣 Alerts read aloud
- Follows, subs, gifted subs, raids, Super Chats / Rants, new members and reward claims: an optional sound plays first, then the voice reads it
  ("Rahul just subscribed!"). Each kind has its own text and sound. A gift bomb or a wave of follow bots is read once.
- Off by default: turn on **Read alerts aloud** on the new page. Reward claims also show in the chat window.

**Tested:** automatic tests with a simulated Twitch (sign-in, rewards, EventSub claims, refunds) and a simulated Kick chat connection.
**Not yet verified on a real channel:** Kick reward claims (Kick's exact event format), creating rewards through Kick's API,
