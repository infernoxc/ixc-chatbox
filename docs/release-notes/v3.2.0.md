## ⬇ How to install (new to IXC)

1. Install **OBS Studio 30 or newer** ([obsproject.com](https://obsproject.com)) and open it once.
2. Below, under **Assets**, click **`IXC-Setup-v3.2.0.exe`** to download it.
3. Open your **Downloads** folder and double-click **`IXC-Setup-v3.2.0.exe`**.
   - **"Windows protected your PC"?** Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. (IXC is free and has no paid signing certificate.
     The file's checksum is in `SHA256SUMS.txt` below.)
4. Click **Express setup**. If OBS is open, close it when the setup asks.
5. IXC opens a **setup assistant**: follow it. It sets up OBS, asks for your channel names, and tests everything.
6. Open **OBS** and go live. The IXC panels are under **Docks**; IXC sits in the tray (bottom right).

**Already have IXC?** Run `IXC-Setup-v3.2.0.exe` the same way. Your settings, accounts, queue and playlists stay (a backup is
made first). Or wait: IXC updates itself when you're not live.

Need help? [📖 Step-by-step guide](https://github.com/infernoxc/ixc-chatbox/blob/main/docs/GUIDE.md) ·
[Something wrong?](https://github.com/infernoxc/ixc-chatbox/blob/main/docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md)
· IXC > Updates & diagnostics > **Fix automatically**.

## What's new in 3.2.0

### 🔧 Fixed
- **Kick chat didn't connect** ("App key … not in this cluster"): Kick retired the chat key IXC used. IXC now uses Kick's current
  key, falls back by itself if Kick changes it again, and remembers what works.
- **The same songs kept repeating** with Shuffle on. Now every song in the queue plays once before any song repeats, and never the
  same song twice in a row. Autoplay skips songs you heard recently, also other uploads of them (lyrics video, "official audio").
- **Phone remote**: the music buttons now switch each platform on or off and show mixes like Twitch + YouTube correctly.
- **YouTube "confirm you're not a bot"**: when YouTube shows this instead of your channel, and your YouTube account is connected,
  IXC asks YouTube's official API for your live stream.

### ✨ New
- **Starts and stops with OBS**: open OBS and IXC is there; close OBS and IXC closes too. Only the tray icon waits in the
  background. (Settings > *Start and stop with OBS* to keep IXC running all the time.)
- **Audio routing** page: switch the **music** and the **chat voice** on or off **per platform**, for example music only on
  Twitch and YouTube. Needs one OBS output per platform (a multistream plugin such as Aitum Multistream), each on its own audio
  track; the page shows which track each platform uses.
- **Ready-made commands** (Commands > + Ready-made commands): `!commands`, `!followage` (Twitch), `!clip` (Twitch clip, or OBS's
  replay buffer), `!discord` and `!socials` (switched off until you add your links).
- **Playlists** and **Recently played** on the Music page.
- Platforms page shows when the last chat message came in and whether IXC can reply there.

### After updating
- To use `!clip` on Twitch, sign in to Twitch again once (Platforms & accounts) to allow clips.
- If you want IXC running without OBS (for example the phone remote only), switch off *Start and stop with OBS* in Settings.

### Tested
- **Automated**, with simulated Twitch, Kick, YouTube and OBS servers and a real browser: 64 tests.
- **Clean Windows machine** with this installer: install, update keeping settings, crash recovery, uninstall, start/stop with OBS
  (with a stand-in OBS program): 38/38.
- **Real platforms**: Twitch chat connected and received messages; Kick chat connected (3 runs).
- **Performance** (test machine, 4 cores): idle 0.03 % CPU, 53 MB RAM; 300 chat messages in 30 s with chat voice: 0.42 % CPU.
- Not yet tested on a real stream: Kick messages and replies, YouTube through the official API, Twitch `!clip` / `!followage`,
  per-platform audio on real multistream outputs, start/stop with a real OBS. If something doesn't work, use IXC > Updates &
  diagnostics > **Export diagnostics** (no passwords inside) and open an issue.

**Not supported:** TikTok (no official chat API), Instagram and Facebook Live (need an app approved by Meta).
