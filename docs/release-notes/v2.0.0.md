**IXC ChatBox 2.0** makes chat TTS reliable, adds a phone remote that works over mobile data, and moves everything into one small native background program, **IXC Core**.

### Highlights
- **TTS that really reads chat.** It now runs inside IXC instead of inside the dock page, so it reads whenever IXC runs (v1 only read while the dock page was open with its own ON switch).
  - *Ignore my account* (found automatically from Streamer.bot), *ignore bots*, a never-speak list and blocked words.
  - Cleanup of links, emotes, emoji, spam and shouting.
  - A message mirrored on several platforms is read once.
  - Queue size, stale-message drop, per-user and per-minute limits, and `!tts` priority.
  - Speed, pitch and volume; history with replay, skip and clear; and a *not read (and why)* list.
- **New voices:** *Neerja Expressive* (Indian female, 8 % faster) and *Madhur* (Indian male, 10 % faster), plus an **offline Windows voice** that switches in automatically when the neural voice service fails.
- **Phone remote from anywhere:** press 📱, scan the QR code, and control chat, TTS and music from your phone, even on mobile data.
  - Encrypted Cloudflare tunnel: no router or firewall changes.
  - One-time pairing codes and expiring sessions; nothing on your PC is reachable without pairing.
- **Diagnostics page** at `http://localhost:8767/diag`.

### Measured (Windows 11, OBS 32.2.2, 60-second test with the usual OBS pages open)
| | CPU | RAM | Requests from OBS pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| v1.0.0 (PowerShell helper + relay) | 0.065 % | 152 MB | ~260 polls/min |
| **v2.0.0 (IXC Core)** | **0.011 %** | **42 MB** | a push every 5 s while music plays |

Stress test: 6,000 chat events at up to ~2,900/s with 4 pages connected. Memory stayed flat (~55 MB), and handles and threads returned to baseline afterwards.

### Upgrading from v1
Close OBS and run the new installer. Your settings are kept, and a backup is saved. The OBS URLs stay the same. v1's TTS ON/voice and `own=` names are taken over the first time the dock opens.
If you had turned on v1's Wi-Fi phone view, see [UNINSTALL.md](https://github.com/infernoxc/ixc-chatbox/blob/main/docs/UNINSTALL.md#update-to-a-new-version) to remove its firewall rule.

### Known limitations
- The neural voices use Microsoft Edge's **unofficial** "Read aloud" service, which can change or stop. The offline voice takes over, and the dock and diagnostics say so.
- *Madhur* is a Hindi voice reading English with an Indian accent; there's only one en-IN male neural voice. Offline Indian voices need Windows' *English (India)* speech pack.
- Cloudflare quick tunnels are meant for testing and have no uptime guarantee. The phone address changes whenever the tunnel restarts (scan a new code).
- In a normal browser tab, the TTS page needs one click before it may play sound (OBS doesn't).
